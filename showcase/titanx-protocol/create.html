The pair

The coin trades against this token on its curve, and the fees come in as that token. They are turned into SOL before they are paid out.

Paid out in

Pick up to ten stocks. The holders' share of the fees buys these stocks and they are sent to every holder by how much of the coin each one holds. Pick none and the holders are paid in SOL.

Dev buy

0.5 SOL of your own coin

Summary

Fees are collected from the curve every couple of minutes.

The coin is launched from the STONKZ wallet, and the tokens from your dev buy are sent to your wallet right after the launch.

A launch usually takes 5 to 20 seconds from the moment you sign. Keep the page open until the coin is live.
