    How TITANX Works

    1. Architect

    Configure your titan basket of up to 10 blue-chip or pre-IPO equities and liquidity pair. Sign one atomic transaction to launch the decentralized index.

    2. Accrue & Swap

    Every swap on the bonding curve captures volume fees into an autonomous, non-custodial yield vault dedicated specifically to your index.

    3. Streamed Dividends

    Accumulated fees automatically acquire the underlying shares and stream them proportionally into holders' wallets with zero manual claims.
